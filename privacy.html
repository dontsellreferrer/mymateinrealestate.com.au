Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

My Mate in Real Estate (MMIRE) operates at mymateinrealestate.com.au and mmire.com.au. We connect private property sellers with trusted local real estate agents, and introduce platform partners to the Referrer.com.au professional referral infrastructure.

What information we collect

We collect only the information needed to connect you with the right people. Depending on how you use the site, this may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your suburb or postcode
  • Your area of professional expertise (for agent registrations)
  • Your company or platform name (for platform partner registrations)

We do not collect payment information, identity documents, or any sensitive personal information.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Connect you with an appropriate local Mate in your area
  • Follow up on your expression of interest as an agent or platform partner
  • Introduce you to Referrer.com.au where relevant

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing to third parties, or automated profiling.

Consent before sharing

Your personal details are never shared with a third party — including a local Mate or Referrer.com.au member — without your explicit consent. Where SMS consent is used, you must reply to confirm before any details are passed on. If you do not respond within 7 days, your details are permanently deleted from our records.

How we store your information

Your data is stored securely in Supabase, a GDPR-compliant cloud database hosted in Australia. Access is restricted to authorised personnel only. We do not store your information on third-party advertising platforms or marketing databases.

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate this platform:

  • Supabase — secure database storage
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Twilio — SMS consent and communication (via Referrer.com.au)
  • Railway — application hosting

Each of these services operates under their own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

Referrer.com.au

Where you choose to join Referrer.com.au — either directly or following a registration on this site — your information will be subject to the Referrer.com.au Privacy Policy in addition to this policy. Referrer.com.au is a separate platform that handles its own compliance, consent, and payment infrastructure.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information at any time
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 5 business days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
