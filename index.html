What is a Mate?

Not an agent.
Not a stranger.
Your Mate.

A Mate is a real estate professional in your suburb — experienced, approved, and on call for when your private sale needs an expert eye. They're not there to take over. They're there to back you up.

Checks your paperwork

Contracts, Section 32s, special conditions — a Mate knows what to look for before you sign anything.

Reads the buyer for you

Serious, tyre-kicker, or lowballer? Your Mate's read a thousand buyers. They'll tell you honestly.

Picks up when it matters

7pm on a Friday, something unexpected happens. Your Mate picks up. That's the whole point.

Takes over if you need them to

If at any point you decide you'd rather hand it over — your Mate can step in as your full agent. Your call.

We've heard it all

"Sounds too good to be true."

Fair. Here's what's actually going on.

"What does the agent get out of it?"

Your Mate earns referral commissions through Referrer.com.au — for loans, property management, and professional services. They don't need to charge you. They're already making the network work for them.

"Will they pressure me to list with them?"

No. MMIRE Mates are approved specifically because they respect the private sale choice. If you want to list with them later, they'll help. But that's your decision, not theirs to push.

"What if my sale is really simple?"

Then you'll barely need them. Most Mates hope your sale goes smoothly. But when something unexpected happens — and in property, something usually does — they're already in your corner.
